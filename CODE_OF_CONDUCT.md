# Project Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

We aim to create a collaborative, inclusive, and supportive environment where all team members can contribute meaningfully to our project. We recognize that everyone has different strengths, working styles, schedules, and levels of experience. Our goal is to complete a high-quality project while ensuring that the process is fair, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone.

## 2. Our Team Values

We agree to:
- **Respect:** Treat each other with courtesy, respect differing opinions, and provide constructive feedback without personal criticism.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for assigned tasks, meet agreed deadlines, and communicate proactively when challenges arise.
- **Collaboration:** Share ideas, knowledge, resources, and feedback instead of working in isolation.
- **Inclusivity:** Ensure that everyone has an opportunity to participate in discussions and decision-making.
- **Transparency:** Be honest about our progress, challenges, contributions, and use of resources, including AI tools.

## 3. Communication and Collaboration

- Communicate respectfully and professionally through our agreed group communication channels.
- Respond to messages within a reasonable timeframe, ideally within 24 hours on weekdays.
- Share updates on task progress and notify the team as early as possible if we anticipate delays.
- Listen to each other's ideas and make space for everyone to contribute.
- Raise concerns early rather than allowing misunderstandings or issues to accumulate.

## 4. Contributions and Responsibilities

- Each team member is expected to contribute fairly to the project, including research, data collection, analysis, coding, visualization, documentation, and presentation, as applicable.
- Tasks and responsibilities will be agreed upon collaboratively and documented so that everyone understands their role.
- Team members should seek help when needed and offer support when others encounter difficulties.
- All team members should review the final deliverables and ensure they understand the overall project, not only their individual contributions.
- Contributions will be acknowledged honestly and fairly.

## 5. Feedback and Conflict Resolution

- Feedback should be constructive, specific, and focused on improving the work rather than criticizing individuals.
- Differences in opinions should be discussed openly and resolved through respectful conversation.
- We will prioritize evidence, project objectives, and agreed criteria when making technical or analytical decisions.
- If disagreements cannot be resolved, we will seek input from the entire group and reach a decision through discussion and consensus where possible.

## 6. Academic Integrity and Use of AI

- All work must comply with the course's academic integrity requirements.
- AI tools may be used responsibly to support brainstorming, debugging, explanations, and editing, provided their use is consistent with course policies.
- Team members are responsible for verifying AI-generated content, code, interpretations, and results before incorporating them into the project.
- We will not present AI-generated content or work as our own without appropriate review, verification, and disclosure where required.
- We will disclose AI use where required by the course and maintain transparency about how it supported our work.

## 7. Handling Breaches

If a team member does not follow this Code of Conduct, we will:
1. Address the concern respectfully and directly whenever appropriate.
2. Discuss the issue as a group and clarify expectations.
3. Agree on corrective actions, responsibilities, and timelines.
4. Follow up to ensure the issue has been resolved and does not continue.

Repeated or serious concerns that cannot be resolved within the group will be escalated to the instructor or course staff when necessary.
